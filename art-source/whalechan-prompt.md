## prompt · 闭嘴常态

```
PERMANENT IDENTITY (locked, do not alter): an ageless humanoid ocean girl. Exactly two arms and two legs. Paired side whale-fin ears — dark navy pointed fins with a pale cyan inner membrane — on both sides of the head. One thin forward-curving ahoge forming a slender loop on the crown. One whale tail ending in a split fluke, emerging naturally from her lower back and integrated with the outfit. Broad round face, large expressive eyes with deep-ocean-blue irises and bright highlights, tiny nose, small mouth, soft rosy cheeks. Deep-ocean-blue hair mass that fades to cyan at the long wavy curled tips. She is NOT a toddler, NOT a baby, NOT a real whale, NOT a mermaid, NOT a half-whale creature.

EXPRESSION: relaxed neutral pose, eyes gently open and looking at the viewer, mouth closed in a small soft smile, calm and sweet.

THEME: cute casual mobile-game mascot, clean readable silhouette, built to work as a 2D game sprite.
PROPORTION: chibi, 2.5 heads tall (measure the head only, ignoring ahoge, headpiece, hair, skirt and tail). Cute and round but not infantile.
COMPOSITION: single character, full body, front view, centered, facing the viewer, feet slightly apart, arms relaxed at her sides.
BACKGROUND: flat solid warm ivory cream #F5EADD, completely plain — no gradient, no scenery, no cast shadow, no vignette.
TEXT: absolutely no text, no watermark, no signature, no logo, no frame, no panel, no grid, no character sheet.
REFERENCE LIMITS: original design only; do not imitate any existing copyrighted character.
ANATOMY: exactly two arms, two legs, one tail, two fin ears, one ahoge, five fingers per hand. Correct limb count.
OUTPUT: one high-resolution illustration, clean crisp edges.

Canonical maid outfit: white frilled lace headpiece resting on the crown of her head; one small blue bow at each side of her head; dark navy long-sleeved dress with muted-gold buttons; white frilled collar; navy chest ribbon with a small blue gem; white frilled apron bearing a small blue whale emblem; navy waistband with gold buttons; navy skirt with a white frilled hem, gold star embroidery and a gold bow; white thigh-high socks with frilled tops; dark blue strap shoes with gold buckles.
```
